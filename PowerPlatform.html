HOLIDAY REQUEST APP

▾

An app for requesting, editing, viewing and accepting/rejecting holiday requests.

The app is in use at multiple companies, the largest having 70+ employees. Holiday balances are by the hour and the app caters for different shift patterns and rota days.

The app features login functionality via a pin/password set by each employee (due to some devices being shared) and helps catalogues users as either a Team Member or Team Manager.

Members can request holiday and edit or view their holiday requests. In addition, Managers can accept/reject holiday requests and view holiday for their specific team (or if part of higher managements/HR) the whole company.

All holiday data is held in SharePoint, with accepted holiday requests appearing on a Calendar view which is also visible in the Company's Microsoft Teams (where the app is also embedded).

SharePoint

I created the below in SharePoint:

  • Holday Data - a list of all employees including their; shift pattern, manager, remaining holiday balance, holiday carried over from the previous year and total holiday balance.
  • Rota Days - a list containing employees who work rota day's and which day(s) of the week they do not work.
  • Public Holidays - a list of all public holidays and (if required) any days the whole company is closed (for example ordinary working days between Christmas and New Year.
  • Requested Holiday - a list of all holiday requests, who requested the holiday, the start and end date, whether the holiday was accepted/rejected and holiday balance to be deducted.
  • Accepted Holiday Expanded - a list of any accepted holiday requests; listing each individual days's holiday between the requested dates, whether its a weekened, rota day or public holiday, whether each of the listed days need to be deducted from an individuals holiday balance and (if applicable) the balance to be deduced for each day
  • Holiday Data - a calendar view of all accepted holiday embedded in Microsoft Teams.

Power Automate

There are multiple flows in Power Automate:

  • On submit of a holiday request (and the request being patched to a SharePoint list) a flow ascertains who an individual's manager is and emails them details of the request, with accept/reject options.

    If accepted:
    • An email is sent to the requestee confirming their holiday has been approved, the request is updated to accepted in SharePoint, and it appears on the Calendar view.


    If rejected:
    • An email is sent to the requestee confirming their holiday has been rejected, the request is updated to rejected in SharePoint, and it appears on the Calendar view.

LONE WORKING AND RISK ASSESSMENT APP

▾

The app enables surveyors to complete a site safesty risk assessment before undertaking a site survey. The questions are dynamic; the questions presented to the surveyor changed based upon previous answers.

If the risk is deemed too great the app instructs users to contact the office for further advice or to cancel the survey altogether.

In addition to the safety questions; the app allows users to take and upload photographs and tag specific risks. If the risk doesn't already exist on a Risk Register SharePoint list then the risk is added so it becomes a permanent addition to the risk options selectable through a drop down within the app.

The app allows users to confirm who they are working with; or if working alone to set a reminder (for 1, 2 or 3 hours based upon risk) to contact and check in with their office.

The client later requested the addition of an Accident Report form; giving users the ability to record and submit details of any accidents direct from site. The form could collect third-party details, a summary of the accident

The client required that an accident location be recorded in the format of What 3 Words. The W3W API proved too costly

I created the below in SharePoint:

  • Risk Assessments List

Power Automate

There are multiple flows in Power Automate:

  • A flow is triggered upon creation of a row on the Risk Assessment list, the flow fetches and attaches any corresponding photos and populates an HTML table that is then emailed to a central mailbox. The flow also checks to see if any risks submitted by the surveyor are unqique; in which case the flow adds them to the Risk Reigster SharePoint List.
  • A flow runs every 5 minutes to check for any 'open' records where the reminder time is in the past (when compared to the current flow start time). For any records that are; a push notification is sent to the user. If a user has complated the job in question there is a cancel nofitifcations option within the app. This updates the record to closed.
  • Another scheduled flow runs at 5:30pm; moves any closed reminder entries for that day to a reminders archive list and emails a list of 'open' reminders to a central mailbox so engineers can be contacted.
  • A flow is triggered upon creation of a row on the Accident Report list, the flow fetches and attaches any corresponding photos and populates an HTML table that is then emailed to a central mailbox.

AGGREGATE REPORTING APP

▾

This Power App was designed as a reporting application, bringing together data from seven separate SharePoint lists into a single, easy-to-use view. By consolidating this information in one place, users can quickly understand the overall picture without needing to navigate between multiple systems or lists.

The customer already uses a suite of Power Apps as part of their day-to-day processes. For that reason, the reporting solution was deliberately built as a Power App, rather than a standalone Power BI report. This ensures a consistent user experience, keeps everything within the same Microsoft 365 ecosystem, and allows the report to sit naturally alongside existing applications.

Power Automate

The report can be exported to PDF on demand using a simple button within the app. When the export button is clicked, it triggers a Power Automate flow that generates a PDF version of the report and automatically sends it to the intended recipient via email. This makes it easy to share up-to-date, formatted reports with stakeholders who may not have access to the app itself.
