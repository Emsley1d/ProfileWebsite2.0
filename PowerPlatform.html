HOLIDAY REQUEST APP

▾

An app for requesting, viewing, deleting/cancelling and accepting/rejecting holiday requests.

The app is in use at multiple companies, the largest having 70+ employees. Holiday balances are by the hour and the app caters for different shift patterns and rota days.

The app features login functionality via a pin/password set by each employee (due to some devices being shared) and helps catalogues users as either a Team Member or Team Manager.

Members can request holiday and edit or view their holiday requests. In addition, Managers can accept/reject holiday requests and view holiday for their specific team (or if part of higher managements/HR) the whole company.

All holiday data is held in SharePoint, with accepted holiday requests appearing on a Calendar view which is also visible in the Company's Microsoft Teams (where the app is also embedded).

For ease of use; the app allows staff to include weekends, public holidays and rota days in their request meaning they do not have to submit multiple for a period which includes any of the aforementioned.

Galleries allow individuals to see any pending and any accepted requests. Team Managers have additional screens allowing them to see all pending and all booked holiday for their teams.

SharePoint

I created the below in SharePoint:

  • Holiday Data - a list of all employees including their; shift pattern, manager, remaining holiday balance, holiday carried over from the previous year and total holiday balance.
  • Rota Days - a list containing employees who work rota day's and which day(s) of the week they do not work.
  • Public Holidays - a list of all public holidays and (if required) any days the whole company is closed (for example ordinary working days between Christmas and New Year.
  • Requested Holiday - a list of all holiday requests, who requested the holiday, the start and end date, whether the holiday was accepted/rejected and holiday balance to be deducted.
  • Accepted Holiday Expanded - a list of any accepted holiday requests; listing each individual days's holiday between the requested dates, whether its a weekened, rota day or public holiday, whether each of the listed days need to be deducted from an individuals holiday balance and (if applicable) the balance to be deduced for each day
  • Holiday Data - a calendar view of all accepted holiday embedded in Microsoft Teams.

Power Automate

There are multiple flows in Power Automate:

  • On submit of a holiday request (and the request being patched to a SharePoint list) a flow ascertains who an individual's manager is and emails them details of the request, with accept/reject options and corresponding emails sent in reply to the individual who requested the holiday..
  • If a holiday request or booked holiday is cancelled, the request/holiday is updated with a deleted status (but not actually deleted - a stipulation from the client) and an email is sent to the individual's manager to advise them of the cancelled holiday.
  • When a holiday request is accepted another flow runs; this adds each individual day of the request to another SharePoint list. The flow checks each day to establish if it's a weekend, a public holiday, an individual's rota day or whether the day is in the current holiday year or the next. If any of these are true then 0 hours are attributed to each day. For all other days another SharePoint list is checked; this includes their shift patterns. Corresponding hours worked are then attributed to each day. Finally the app runs through all days booked for that request, totals the hours against each day and deducts them from a user's remaining balance.

ALCOHOL AND DRUG SCREENING APP

▾

An app for requesting alcohol/drug tests and for recording the results. Created for a company running a fleet of vehichles.

Immediate tests were requested at the time of an accident or if there were suspicions around a driver. Planned tests were for screening new employees or for scheduling random tests.

The Tester Information form captured; who was carrying out the test, who was being tested, any medication/information a driver wished to disclose, confirmation photo ID was provided, whether the driver consented to the test and, if they did, whether or not it was negative.

Power Automate

There are multiple flows in Power Automate:

  • Upon creation of a test request; a flow is triggered to notify relevant parties. Different groups of users were emailed depeneding on the test type. All emails were sent simultaneously to ensure the individuals towards the top of each list weren't shouldering the burden of completing the majority of tests. Upon the first acceptance (via email buttons) all subsequent attempts to accept failed. The individual who accepted the test was then emailed further details of the test; helping to protect the identity of the individual to be tested and preventing any potential favourtism. Once the test has been accepted, the individual who requested the test receives an email to confirm it has been accepted with the details of the individual completing the test included. If the test in question was planned then an entry was added to a SharePoint calendar.
  • Once a test had been completed a flow was triggered to notify; the driver, their manager, the tester and HR. The entry for the test was then deleted from SharePoint (from a list with already strict access) to help protect the indentity and integrity of the driver.

TIMESHEET & LOCATION REGISTER APP

▾

The app enables employees to clock in and out when arriving at different offices and acts as a site register; in the event of a fire or site evacuation etc.

The app is use at one company but across a number of their sites; each version of app is set to a different site location.

A user simply enters their unique pin to clock in or out; if a user isn't currently clocked in they are done so upon input of their pin and vice versa if they are already clocked in at the point of entering their pin. A user is then signed out and the screen reverts to the home screen ready for a new user to click in or out. This is designed to keep interaction to a minimum to prevent users queing at the tablets.

Managers and Fire Wardens have a different level of access (managed via a SharePoint List). When Managers log in they have access to a settings screen where they can change the set location of the tablet. Fire Wardens have access to a screen that lists all individuals at the office/site that are currently clocked in.

LONE WORKING AND RISK ASSESSMENT APP

▾

The app enables surveyors to complete a site safesty risk assessment before undertaking a site survey. The questions are dynamic; the questions presented to the surveyor changed based upon previous answers.

If the risk is deemed too great the app instructs users to contact the office for further advice or to cancel the survey altogether.

In addition to the safety questions; the app allows users to take and upload photographs and tag specific risks. If the risk doesn't already exist on a Risk Register SharePoint list then the risk is added so it becomes a permanent addition to the risk options selectable through a drop down within the app.

The app allows users to confirm who they are working with; or if working alone to set a reminder (for 1, 2 or 3 hours based upon risk) to contact and check in with their office.

The client later requested the addition of an Accident Report form; giving users the ability to record and submit details of any accidents direct from site. The form could collect third-party details, a summary of the accident

The client required that an accident location be recorded in the format of What 3 Words. The W3W API proved too costly

AGGREGATE REPORTING APP

▾

This Power App was designed as a reporting application, bringing together data from seven separate SharePoint lists into a single, easy-to-use view. By consolidating this information in one place, users can quickly understand the overall picture without needing to navigate between multiple systems or lists.

The customer already used a suite of Power Apps as part of their day-to-day processes. For that reason, the reporting solution was deliberately built as a Power App, rather than a standalone Power BI report.

Power Automate

The report can be exported to PDF on demand using the 'EXPORT TO PDF' button. When the export button is clicked, it triggers a Power Automate flow that generates a PDF version of the report and automatically sends it to the intended recipient via email. This makes it easy to share up-to-date, formatted reports with stakeholders who may not have access to the app itself.
